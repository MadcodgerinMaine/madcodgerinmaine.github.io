Stock Jug Recipes (1-gallon jugs, 100× concentrate)

Jug A — Masterblend + Epsom: 378 g Masterblend 5-11-26 + 189 g Epsom salt. Fill with warm water, shake until fully dissolved.

Jug B — Calcium nitrate: 378 g calcium nitrate. Fill with water, shake.

To use: ml of stock = grams in the recipe × 10, per gallon of water. Add Jug A first, swirl, then Jug B. Never mix A and B together undiluted.

Storage: 6+ months sealed, cool and dark. Each jug makes 100+ gallons of working solution. If crystals form in a cold shed, warm the jug and shake.
