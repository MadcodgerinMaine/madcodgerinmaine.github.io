My Google Sheets PV Tracker

We’ve had access to the Google suite of office programs (I’m supposed to say “apps” to not sound really old, right?) for years now. It comes “free” (Ha!) with our email hosting on the paid version of Gmail, but we’ve never really used it because we’ve used the similar Microsoft suite of solutions since we were young. So, for a very long time. But I keep hearing about how nicely Google Sheets works with various online and cloud-hosted programs (yeah, apps) and doing something similar with Microsoft seems to require use of their OneDrive or Teams or Sharepoint services, all of which I completely despise. So, I’m giving Google Sheets a try in hopes of gaining some skill with it. What better way to do that than to keep track of our solar data? If you can actually see this when you click the link, I guess I figured it out!

You can learn more in the Tools & Apps I use section, including a link there that will allow you to download a copy of the sheet (file) I created.
